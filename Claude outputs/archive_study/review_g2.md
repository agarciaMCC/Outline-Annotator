# Archive study, group 2: six past soffit plans vs the Dim Soffit v2 rules

Read-only review of issued PDFs (rendered with PyMuPDF). Crops are in `Claude outputs/archive_study/g2/`
(`<job>_full.png` is each whole sheet).

## 1078 O Street Office: 1.3.1 Level 3 Soffit Plan (1/8" = 1'-0", 2019)
- Plain rectangular office plate, two cores. Slab edge is located off the nearest grid on each side, at both
  ends (1'-10 1/2" off grid 1 at top and bottom, 4'-5 1/2" off grid A/F) (`1078_topedge.png`, `1078_bottom.png`).
- Cores are dimensioned as **chains of wall pieces and gaps** (5'-6" | 5'-2" | 9'-0" | 13'-6" | 2'-4" | 12'-3" | 8'-1")
  with the 55'-10" overall outside: walls are dimensioned, and dims run across the shaft areas (`1078_rightcore.png`).
  The left stair core does the same (13'-4" | 6'-8" | 1'-3" | 10'-1", 31'-4" overall outside) (`1078_leftcore.png`).
- Beams PB-01 (60x16) are centred on grids, yet each gets a 2'-6" | 2'-6" width split and a 5'-0" overall at one
  end, plus a 25'-0" beam-to-beam chain (`1078_topedge.png`). The offset beam PB-02 is located at one end only
  (3'-9", 2'-4", 1'-5") (`1078_rightedge.png`).
- The placing-boom blockout is located 30'-4" / 33'-8" off grid B, one of the few locating dims over 30 ft
  (`1078_leftcore.png`). Small blockouts are located near edge + size (1'-7" | 2'-1" | 5'-3 1/4").
- Blue notes call out scope: "CURBS AROUND OPENINGS NOT WITHIN MCC SCOPE OF WORK" (`1078_rightedge.png`).
- Grid-to-grid strings + 210'-0" / 142'-0" overall on all 4 sides.

## 1048 Seattle U Housing: 1.11 Level 11 (Roof) Soffit Plan & Details (2018, scale not printed)
- Angled north edge is dimensioned on its **own angled grid set** (1A–6F), with an angled grid string + 102'-1"
  overall and a 3'-7 1/2" offset from the angled grid line (`1048_angled.png`).
- Small openings: near edge off the closest grid + size, chained (1'-10" | 10"; 1'-0" | 3'-0" | 9'-3"), on both
  axes (`1048_smallopngs.png`). Stair-2 openings inside the core are located off wall faces (`1048_stair2.png`).
- Left edge notches use a **grid | edge | edge | grid chain** (10'-0 1/2" | 13'-2" | 5'-5") with the 28'-7 1/2"
  grid-to-grid outside it, rather than stacks (`1048_elevleft.png`).
- Text labels on tiny dims: "6 1/2" TO GRID", "1/2" EOW", "8" EOS", "8" BEAM" (`1048_stair2.png`).
- The tower-crane opening's 10'-0" x 10'-0" size sits inside the large hole marked "LEVEL-10 SLAB EDGE BELOW"
  (`1048_smallopngs.png`). The drop slab along grid G has its 12'-3" width dimensioned inside the drop (`1048_bottom.png`).
- A CJ runs between grids C and D; no locating dim to it was found. Grid strings + overall on 2 sides only
  (left, bottom), plus the angled string.

## 1128 Sky Ala Moana: 1.2 Second Floor Soffit Plan (3/32" = 1'-0")
- Large podium plate. Slab edge is located off the closest grid (6" off A, 1'-8 1/2" off grid 1), and walls/
  beams near the edge are stacked off grid B (13'-7 7/8" | 8") (`1128_topleft.png`).
- CJ located 3'-7" off grid at its west end only, with "JOG C.J. AROUND COLUMN" and "NON-STRESS CONSTR. JOINT"
  notes, and the CJ jog located 6'-1" / 12'-6 5/8" off the grid (`1128_cj.png`).
- The 48x36 beam (centred on the grid, and the sheet note says so) still gets a 2'-0" | 2'-0" + 4'-0" width
  stack, at one end only; the beam end is located 1'-0" past the grid (`1128_beam.png`).
- Elevator cores: dims run **through the shafts** (8'-2" | 8'-0" | 8'-2", also 9'-0" in the angled core)
  (`1128_rightcores.png`, `1128_angledcore.png`). The angled stair/elevator core is dimensioned along its own angle.
- Edge treatment notes everywhere: "DRIP EDGE", "END DRIP EDGE", "CHAMFER CORNER NO DRIP EDGE"; pour names
  ("POUR 1–5") in boxes (`1128_bottom.png`).
- Grid strings + overall on 3 sides (top, left, right); none at the bottom (`1128_bottomleft.png`).

## 1145 Park Lane Hotel: 1.2.1 Level-2 (Soffit Plan) (3/32" = 1'-0", 2021)
- L-shaped plan with two angled wings, each on its **own angled grid set** with its own angled strings

- Wide beams (120"x24", 57.5"x24") are located off the grid (4'-0" / 6'-0"); slab edge 4'-1 1/2" + 8" off grid,

- Grid strings + overall on all sides of both wings.

## 1285 The Hudson: 1.2.1 Level-2 (Soffit Plan) (1/8" = 1'-0", 2025)
- Simple rectangular plate. Stair/elevator cores are **not dimensioned at all**: a dashed callout sends them
  to enlarged plans (4.1.0 / 4.1.1 / 4.1.2) (`1285_leftopngs.png`, `1285_rightopngs.png`).
- The one complex area (sloped/stepped 38 1/2" PT slab beside the CJ on grid 8) is dimensioned in **red**,
  with angle labels (77°, 83°, 84°, 97°) and chains with the overall outside (2'-8" | 1'-8" | ... | 3'-4 3/4",

- Grid strings on top and bottom only; very short dims (10", 1'-1 1/2", 3 1/2") are pulled off with leaders

- No beams, no small openings on this level.

## 1225 Alder 9: 1.4.0A Level-4 Soffit Plan (From Control) (1/8" = 1'-0", 2024)
- Closest match to v2's approach: every small opening is located with **stacks from the grid** (near edge + size
  joined, far edge as the longer row outside: 10'-6 3/8" | 2'-0", 12'-6 3/8"), on both axes (`1225_leftbay.png`).
- But stacks often run from a **far grid** and grow to **4–5 rows**: 17'-8 1/2" / 20'-0 1/2" / 21'-0" / 21'-9"
  off grid F to openings that sit 1'-2" off grid D (`1225_topright.png`). An opening near grid 3 is located 27'-10 1/8"
  / 29'-10 1/8" off grid 4 (`1225_bottom.png`).
- Openings are marked "(R.O.)" on dims (8'-8" (R.O.), 6'-11" (R.O.)) and in pink X'd blockouts (`1225_elevators.png`).
- Small dims at core walls run from the wall face (2'-3", 7") (`1225_southstair.png`). The 15'-7" / 3'-0" (R.O.)
  dims sit just inside the top of the stair blockouts (`1225_northstair.png`).
- Grid strings + overall + partial overall rows (64'-10 1/2", 37'-0") on all 4 sides; slab edge 9" off grid at both ends.

## Habits not in the rules
- **Columns are never dimensioned** on any sheet, but every column gets a size label (24", 16x24 COL) and an
  elevation box (T/B/COL HT). Notes such as "ALL COLUMNS / BEAMS ARE CENTERED ON GRID LINES U.N.O." appear on
  1078, 1128, 1145, 1285 and 1225.
- **Centred beams still get a width dim** at one end (1078, 1128), even with the centred-on-grid note. That end is
  usually by the slab edge or by a column. No halfway width dim was seen on any long beam.
- **Angled wings use their own grid set**, with their own angled grid strings and overall (1048, 1145, 1128 core).
  Angles are labelled in degrees on the newest sheet (1285).
- **Opening labels:** "OPNG", "STAIR OPNG", "(R.O.)" (rough opening, 1225), "BLOCKOUT", "PLACING BOOM BLOCKOUT",
  "TOWER CRANE OPENING"; an X through the opening (red/pink on newer sheets); triangle tags for penetrations
  ("M05 OPEN", "G0165 CLOSED").
- **Text-only locators on tiny offsets:** "TO GRID", "EOW" (edge of wall), "EOS" (edge of slab), "N. OF GL U".
- **Edge-treatment notes:** "DRIP EDGE", "END DRIP EDGE", "CHAMFER CORNER NO DRIP EDGE", "ADD 3/4" DRIP STRIP".
- **Scope notes in blue** (1078: curbs "NOT WITHIN MCC SCOPE"). **Red revision clouds** (1145) and **red dims**
  for a late-changed area (1285).
- **Pour names in boxes** ("POUR 1"–"POUR 5", 1128) and CJ notes ("JOG C.J. AROUND COLUMN").
- **Cores sent to enlarged plans** with a dashed rounded callout (1285), instead of being dimensioned on the soffit plan.
- Every sheet has slab elevation boxes (T/B/thickness) and section/elevation markers spread over the plan.

## Older vs newer sheets
- 2018–2021 (1048, 1078, 1128, 1145): cores chained wall by wall with dims through the shafts; chains more than stacks.
- 2024–2025 (1225, 1285): openings kept clean (cores to enlarged plans in 1285); stacks from grids with (R.O.) labels
  in 1225. Colour is used for openings (pink) and late changes (red). 1225 lets stacks grow to 4–5 rows off a far grid.
