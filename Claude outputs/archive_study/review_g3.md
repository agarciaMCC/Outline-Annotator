## 1061 SCU South Residences: 1.1.0 LEVEL 1 SOFFIT PLAN (1:160, i.e. 3/64" = 1'-0"), 2019
- A long straight bar plus an angled wing with its own grid set. Grid strings run on all four sides of the bar (bottom overall 288'-11"; sides 78'-5 1/4" and 85'-2 1/4"). The angled wing has its own strings along its angled grids, with 1'-0" offsets from grid to edge (`scu_leftend.png`, `scu_rightend.png`, `scu_top.png`).
- Most slab edges sit on grids, so few locating dims are needed. Openings for open stairs are located off **stair wall faces** (1'-10", 3'-1 1/2", 9'-6", 8'-11"), and walls carry T.O.W. (top of wall) tags. Dims also sit **inside** the open stairs (5'-1 7/8", 4'-8 1/8", 5'-2", 5'-4") (`scu_leftend.png`, `scu_rightend.png`).
- Interior opening (6'-11" x 19'-4"): only its size is shown, on two adjoining sides, with no locating dim nearby (`scu_center.png`).
- Sloped (warped) slab: slope-break points are located with long dims (44'-2", 37'-3 3/8", 37'-0 3/4", 25'-8 3/4"), all over 30 ft. Notes such as "TO L.9", "COLUMN ON L.9" and "TOS STEP 5/8" S OF GRID H". Many 3-line elevation tags (top / bottom / COL HT) and "T:/B:" boxes (`scu_wing.png`).
- Drop caps get their size (11'-0" x 11'-0") on one side per direction, with no locating dim. Note 1 reads "COLUMNS AND DROPCAPS CENTERED ON GRIDS U.N.O." (unless noted otherwise). Numbered "CLOSED" triangles mark closed sleeves/blockouts.

## 1053 Burlingame Point: CW-1.2.C WEST CRANEWAY LEVEL-1 (POUR-27), 2019
- A dense beam grid. Note 1 reads "BEAMS & COLUMNS ARE CENTERED ON GRIDLINES; UNLESS INDICATED OTHERWISE". Grid-centred beams get no dims. Off-grid beams are located by **one chain across the bay at mid-span**: grid | 1'-4" | 12'-10" | 1'-8" beam | 12'-10" | 2'-8" beam. Beam widths sit inside that chain, not at the beam ends (`burl_cjtop.png`, `burl_cjbottom.png`).
- CJs (construction joints) are located off a grid with **red dims matching the red CJ line** (11'-8" / 5'-10", 24'-6"). "CONST JOINT" / "POUR 11 / POUR 27" labels name the pours on each side (`burl_cjbottom.png`, `burl_dropcap.png`).
- Drop panels are not dimensioned. One note covers them all: "9'-0" X 9'-0" X 16" TALL INVERTED DROP CAP ... DIMENSIONS SIMILAR (UNO) ALONG GRID K @ 3, 4, 5..." (`burl_dropcap.png`).
- The west edge is stair/bent-beam bays: a stacked vertical string per bay (1'-8" beam / 5'-9 1/8" / 1'-8"...), with subtotals 32'-6" and 30'-0" outside (`burl_westedge.png`).
- Colour-coded beams (by soffit elevation), BSS (beam soffit step) labels, a camber symbol with an amount, and a notes block (`burl_notes.png`).

## Habits not in the rules
- **Typical notes in place of repeated dims:** "DIMENSIONS SIMILAR (UNO) ALONG GRID K @ 3, 4, 5...", "DIMENSIONS TYP ON PILASTERS ALONG GL A U.N.O.", "TYPICAL IN PILASTERS ALONG PERIMETER U.N.O.", "SIM" next to section marks. One instance is dimensioned and the rest are covered by a note (Burlingame, Platform 16).
- **"Centered on grid U.N.O."** general notes on every sheet (columns, drop caps, beams), so grid-centred items get no dims.
- **Labels:** R.O. / RO on stair, shaft and opening sizes (Dexter, L300); OPNG on small openings; "TO CJ"; "GAP" for slivers; "EDGE OF OPENING ON GRID F" written instead of a zero dim; red "TRANSITION < 3'-0" THIS LOC." notes.
- **Elevation tags everywhere:** 2-box and 3-box tags (top / bottom / COL HT or H=) at every bay or beam. Two styles: Burlingame uses relative elevations (+13'-4"), L300 uses absolute ones (396'-2"). Camber symbol with an amount (Burlingame).
- **Colour:** red for CJs, their dims and pour labels (Burlingame); yellow highlight on revised dims (Dexter); beam colour/hatch by soffit elevation (Burlingame); pink/red for pads.
- **Delay strips / closure pours** (hatched, 7-DAY / 28-DAY) are dimensioned like elements (width + location) (Dexter, L300).
- **Cores and stairs are dimensioned off wall faces, including wall thickness and dims inside the core**, on 4 of 6 sheets. Rules 2, 9 and 10 are the most often broken, but mostly at cores/stairs (rule 13's core exception covers part of this).
- **Angled edges** are located by short dims to a corner point plus an angle (82 deg) (Butte), or by a separate grid set with its own strings (SCU).
- **Match lines / "SEE: 1 / 1.2.2"** heavy dashed lines at pour and zone boundaries. A key plan and 3D view are on most sheets.
- **Older vs newer:** the 2019 sheets (SCU, Burlingame) lean on elevation tags, colour and one-chain-per-bay. The 2022-2024 sheets (Dexter, L300, Butte, Platform 16) use the standard symbol legend, chain + overall pairs and "TO CJ" / "TYP ... U.N.O." notes more consistently.
