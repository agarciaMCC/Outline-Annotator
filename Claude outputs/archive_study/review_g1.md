## 1051 OSU Forestry Complex — 1.1.1 LEVEL-1 (SOFFIT PLAN) — 1" = 10'-0", Oct 2017
- Most locating dims run **from a wall face or beam face, not a grid**: clear spans 17'-2 1/8", 18'-2 1/8",
  34'-2", 35'-2", 13'-2", 14'-8" across the slab field (`osu_bot.png`, `osu_mid.png`). The link to the grid
  is a **note** instead: "WALL FACE 10" OFF GRID", "WALL FACE 2" OFF GRID", "2" NORTH OF GL", "BEAM ON GRID LINE".
- Dims float in the middle of the slab bays, not at the edges; 34'-2" and 35'-2" are over the 30 ft limit.
- Drop caps: located off the column grid, 4'-0" | 4'-0" each side of the grid, plus stacked 8'-0" / 16'-0" off a
  beam face (`osu_bot.png`, `osu_mid.png`).
- Beams on grid still get **half-widths each side of the grid** (2'-0" | 2'-0") at several points along the
  48" beam, despite the "BEAM ON GRID LINE" note.
- Stacks from one face are common (6'-10 3/4" / 8'-10 3/4" / 19'-2"; 21'-0" / 21'-10 1/8") (`osu_top.png`).
- Grid strings: top (bays + 70'-0" overall) and one vertical string down the plan.

## 1077 Rainier Square — 1.1 LEVEL 1 - SOFFIT PLAN — 1/8" = 1'-0", Feb 2019
- West third (grids G–J) is fully dimensioned; **the east two thirds are almost undimensioned**: core openings,
  two small openings near grid 3.5, the zig-zag CJ and slab drops between L–Q have no locating dims
  (`rainier_core.png`, `rainier_right.png`).
- Mix of **chains grid-to-grid** (H | 1'-7" | 7'-0 1/2" | 6'-11 1/2" | J; G | 7'-6" | 6'-8" | 2'-7") and **stacks**
  (opening 9'-1" / 10'-4" off grid 1.1; overall 11'-2 1/2" outside its chain 4'-2 3/4" | 6'-11 3/4")
  (`rainier_botleft.png`, `rainier_topleft.png`).
- Opening near grid 2.5: 5'-7" off the grid then 6'-0" size end to end (size joins locating dim).
- CJ located off grid J and K (9'-0" | 5'-7", 7'-1", 14'-3" to K) — no "TO CJ" text, CJ line is labelled "CJ".
- Sump: dims **inside** the pit outline (8'-5", 9'-10 1/2", 3'-2", 3'-6"). Transfer wall thickness 2'-0" dimensioned.
- Beams labelled by size only ("24 X 48", "72 X 20"); no width dims (note 2: "all vertical elements centred on
  grid lines UNO"). Grid strings top and right (with overalls 164'-0" and 103'-0 1/2").

## Habits not in the rules
- **Elevation box at every bay/beam/column**: 3 stacked lines (T / B / HT or SH: shore height) with the slab/beam
  depth beside it; columns get "C2 - 18X24 COL" + COL HT box. Present on all 6 sheets.
- **Opening size written inside the opening** (Waimanu, Intuit, Kaiser) — a standing habit, not an exception.
- **Notes in place of dims**: "WALL FACE 10" OFF GRID", "2" NORTH OF GL", "BEAM ON GRID LINE", "BEAM ALIGNMENT
  CENTERED ON GRID", "BEAM CENTERED ON COLUMNS LOCATION ABOVE" (OSU, Waimanu).
- **Beam width split about the grid** (3'-0" | 3'-0" + 6'-0", or 2'-0" | 2'-0") even for centred beams.
- Labels: "OPNG"/"OPEN" with an X, "TOS STEP" / "SOFFIT STEP" arrows, "SLAB JOG", "TYP", "U.N.O.", "28 DAY
  CLOSURE STRIP", "DELAY SLAB", "TOWER CRANE OPENING", "CRANE BLOCKOUT", "SWALE", "15" SLAB" boxes, beam size
  labels ("16-18X36 BM", "24 X 48").
- Drop/step depth shown with a hatched step symbol and numbers each side (17 1/2", 8 3/4").
- Small perimeter offsets (4", 8", 1'-11 1/2") from grid to slab edge, often tacked onto the grid-string row.
- Angled parts dimensioned in their own rotated frame, aligned dims along the angled grid.
- RFI triangles ("G504 CLOSED"), revision clouds, open questions on issued sheets.
- **Older vs newer**: OSU (2017) locates from faces with notes and floats dims mid-bay; Rainier (2019) only
  partly dimensioned; 2020–2023 sheets locate off grids more consistently and Dell (2023) adds 3-row strings on
  4 sides. Intuit (2022) doubles as a wall plan (walls, cores, columns all dimensioned).
